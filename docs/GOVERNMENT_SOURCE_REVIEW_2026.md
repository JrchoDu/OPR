# Philippine payroll source review — 2026

Research date: **2026-10-07**. Scope: ordinary private-sector employees on monthly or semi-monthly payroll. The sources below drive the active sample rule bundle in `config/ph/2026`. They were reviewed for this implementation and are checked again by the Java loader against exact expected values and table boundaries. This is a software source review, not an independent payroll/accounting audit or government certification.

## Calculation scope and limits

- The bundle targets private-sector employees in the employer/employee SSS category. It does not apply the household-worker, self-employed, voluntary, OFW, government/GSIS, minimum-wage-earner, or other special schedules.
- Semi-monthly inputs are one paycheck's earnings. Monthly statutory contribution values are divided in half for each of two equal paychecks. This does not handle a split payroll with uneven dates/amounts or month-end reconciliation.
- Basic pay, overtime, and taxable allowance are the only earnings fields. All three are treated as taxable. PhilHealth uses basic pay; SSS and Pag-IBIG use total entered earnings.
- Other deductions reduce net pay after withholding tax. Loans, absences, partial months, non-taxable or de-minimis benefits, 13th-month pay, MWE exemptions, bonuses and commissions beyond these fields, and year-end annualization are not modeled.
- The UI returns a calculation breakdown but does not yet save payroll entries, generate payslips, finalize payroll, or produce statutory remittance reports.

## Rule bundle checks

The Java loader refuses missing rows, any row not marked `YES`, altered SSS share/bracket values, invalid PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG values, and incomplete or changed BIR brackets. The COBOL engine also fails closed on a missing bracket, invalid frequency, malformed request, or deductions exceeding gross pay. Integration fixtures cover a monthly and semi-monthly example. Update the source review, CSV data, exact validation, and fixtures together when an agency changes a rule.
