# Pag-IBIG engine

`PAGIBIG.CBL` caps the monthly fund salary, applies the employee's 1% rate through ₱1,500 and 2% rate above ₱1,500, then calculates the employer's 2% share. The cap, rates, and threshold are passed from the validated CSV bundle. In semi-monthly mode, each monthly share is divided by two. Source and scope are documented in `GOVERNMENT_SOURCE_REVIEW_2026.md`.
