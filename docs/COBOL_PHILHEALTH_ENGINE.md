# PhilHealth engine

`PHILHEALTH.CBL` bounds the employee's basic monthly salary at ₱10,000 and ₱100,000, applies the 5% premium rate, and returns employee and employer halves. The exact schedule is loaded from the validated source bundle. Semi-monthly payroll divides the monthly shares by two. Scope and source are in `GOVERNMENT_SOURCE_REVIEW_2026.md`.
